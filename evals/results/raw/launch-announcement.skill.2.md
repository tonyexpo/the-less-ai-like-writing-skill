# Introducing Kestrel: Project Management Built for How Teams Actually Work Today

Today we're announcing Kestrel, a new project management platform built to help teams move faster, communicate better, and stay aligned without the usual friction.

## Why Now

Modern teams juggle more moving pieces than before: shifting priorities, cross-functional stakeholders, an always-on pace of work. Simplicity, clarity, and speed used to be nice-to-haves. Now they're table stakes, and that's the gap Kestrel was built to close.

## What Makes Kestrel Different

Rather than making teams choose between power and usability, Kestrel offers both. Three principles guided what we built:

- **Clarity over clutter.** Every view surfaces what matters most, so teams spend less time searching and more time doing.
- **Flexibility over rigidity.** Whether a team works in boards, timelines, or lists, Kestrel adapts to how people already work, rather than the other way around.
- **Speed over friction.** From onboarding to everyday use, we prioritized a smooth, intuitive experience at every step.

It's not about adding more features. It's about making the right features effortless to use.

## Designed to Grow With You

Kestrel is built to scale from a small team just getting organized to a larger organization coordinating complex initiatives. That adaptability, not rigid structure, is what helps teams stay focused as their needs evolve.

We can't wait to see what teams build with it.
