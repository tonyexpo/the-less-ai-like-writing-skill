# Introducing Kestrel: Project Management Built for How Teams Actually Work Today

Today we're announcing Kestrel, a new project management platform built to help teams move faster, communicate better, and stay aligned without the friction that usually gets in the way.

## Why Now

Modern teams juggle more moving pieces than ever: shifting priorities, cross-functional stakeholders, and an always-on pace of work. Simplicity, clarity, and speed aren't nice-to-haves anymore—they're table stakes. Kestrel was built from the ground up to meet that moment.

## What Makes Kestrel Different

Rather than forcing teams to choose between power and usability, Kestrel offers both. Three principles guided everything we built:

- **Clarity over clutter.** Every view surfaces what matters most, so teams spend less time searching and more time doing.
- **Flexibility over rigidity.** Whether a team prefers boards, timelines, or lists, Kestrel adapts to how people already work, not the other way around.
- **Speed over friction.** From onboarding to everyday use, we prioritized a smooth, intuitive experience at every step.

It's not about adding more features—it's about making the right features effortless to use. That's what we think will set Kestrel apart.

## Designed to Grow With You

Whether you're a small team just getting organized or a larger organization coordinating complex initiatives, Kestrel is built to scale with you. That adaptability, not rigid structure, is what helps teams stay focused as their needs evolve.

## Looking Ahead

This launch is just the beginning. As teams navigate a fast-changing world of work, we're committed to listening, refining, and building tools that make collaboration genuinely easier. Kestrel is more than a product launch—it's a step toward a future where great work isn't slowed down by the tools meant to support it.

We can't wait to see what teams build with it.
