# Introducing Kestrel: A Project Management Platform for Distributed Teams

Today we're launching Kestrel, a new project management platform built to help teams move faster, communicate better, and stay aligned.

## Why Now

Teams today are more distributed, more deadline-driven, and juggling more tools than they used to. Priorities shift, stakeholders span multiple functions, and the pace rarely lets up. Kestrel was built for that reality from the ground up.

## What Makes Kestrel Different

Kestrel doesn't ask teams to choose between power and usability. Three things guided how we built it:

- **Clarity over clutter.** Every view surfaces what matters most, so teams spend less time searching and more time doing.
- **Flexibility over rigidity.** Whether a team works in boards, timelines, or lists, Kestrel adapts to how people already work.
- **Speed over friction.** From onboarding to daily use, we optimized for a smooth, intuitive experience.

The goal wasn't to add more features. It was to make the right ones effortless to use.

## Built to Scale

Kestrel works for a small team just getting organized and for a larger organization coordinating complex initiatives. It adapts to a team's needs as those needs change, rather than forcing a fixed structure on them.

We're looking forward to seeing what teams build with it.
