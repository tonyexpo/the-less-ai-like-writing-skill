# Migration Moment: Why the Postgres 12 → 16 Upgrade Matters Now

On the 14th, we're moving from Postgres 12 to Postgres 16.

## What's Happening

**Upgrade date: the 14th.** Mark it now to avoid friction later.

**Write freeze: roughly 20 minutes.** If you're pushing a hotfix or wrapping up a feature branch, plan around this window.

**Read replicas: staying up.** Writes pause, but reads carry on — dashboards, reports, and read-heavy services keep running largely undisturbed.

## Why This Approach

The priorities here are minimal disruption, clear communication, and forward compatibility.

More details will follow as the date approaches. Thanks for adapting alongside the systems we all depend on.
