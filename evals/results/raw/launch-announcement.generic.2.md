# Introducing Kestrel: Project Management for How Teams Actually Work

Today we're launching Kestrel, a new project management platform built to help teams move faster, communicate better, and stay aligned.

## Why Now

Teams today juggle more than ever: shifting priorities, cross-functional stakeholders, and an always-on pace of work. Simplicity, clarity, and speed aren't nice-to-haves in that environment—they're requirements. Kestrel was built for that reality.

## What Makes Kestrel Different

Kestrel doesn't force teams to choose between power and usability. Three principles guided what we built:

- **Clarity over clutter.** Every view surfaces what matters most, so teams spend less time searching and more time doing.
- **Flexibility over rigidity.** Whether a team prefers boards, timelines, or lists, Kestrel adapts to how people already work.
- **Speed over friction.** From onboarding to daily use, we prioritized a smooth, intuitive experience at every step.

The goal isn't more features. It's making the right features effortless to use.

## Designed to Grow With You

Whether you're a small team just getting organized or a larger organization coordinating complex initiatives, Kestrel scales with you. That adaptability, not rigid structure, is what helps teams stay focused as their needs change.

## Looking Ahead

This launch is just the start. We'll keep listening, keep refining, and keep building tools that make collaboration easier. We're looking forward to seeing what teams build with Kestrel.
