# Migration Moment: Why the Postgres 12 → 16 Upgrade Matters Now

On the 14th, we're moving from Postgres 12 to Postgres 16. As our systems scale, staying current with the platforms that power them isn't optional.

## What's Happening

**Upgrade date: the 14th.** Mark it now so there's less friction later.

**Write freeze: roughly 20 minutes.** Short and scoped. If you're pushing a hotfix or wrapping up a feature branch, plan around this window.

**Read replicas: staying up.** Writes pause, but reads carry on, so dashboards, reports, and read-heavy services should keep running largely undisturbed.

## Why This Approach

The migration is built around minimal disruption, clear communication, and forward compatibility.

## Looking Ahead

The 14th is a step, not a finish line. More details will follow as the date approaches. Thanks for adapting alongside the systems we all depend on.
