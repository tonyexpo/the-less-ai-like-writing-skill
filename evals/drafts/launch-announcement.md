# Introducing Kestrel: Project Management Built for How Teams Actually Work Today

In a world where teams are more distributed, more deadline-driven, and more overwhelmed by tools than ever before, the way we manage projects has never mattered more. Today, we're excited to announce Kestrel, a new project management platform designed to help teams move faster, communicate better, and stay aligned—without the friction that so often gets in the way.

## Why Now

Modern teams juggle more moving pieces than ever: shifting priorities, cross-functional stakeholders, and an always-on pace of work. That's precisely why simplicity, clarity, and speed aren't just nice-to-haves anymore—they're table stakes. Kestrel was built from the ground up to meet that moment.

## What Makes Kestrel Different

Rather than forcing teams to choose between power and usability, Kestrel offers both. Three principles guided everything we built:

- **Clarity over clutter.** Every view is designed to surface what matters most, so teams spend less time searching and more time doing.
- **Flexibility over rigidity.** Whether a team prefers boards, timelines, or lists, Kestrel adapts to the way people already work, not the other way around.
- **Speed over friction.** From onboarding to everyday use, we've prioritized a smooth, intuitive experience at every step.

It's not about adding more features—it's about making the right features effortless to use. That distinction, we believe, is what will set Kestrel apart.

## Designed to Grow With You

Whether you're a small team just getting organized or a larger organization coordinating complex initiatives, Kestrel is built to scale alongside you. It's this adaptability—not rigid structure—that helps teams stay focused as their needs evolve.

## Looking Ahead

We see this launch as just the beginning. As teams continue to navigate a fast-changing world of work, our commitment is to keep listening, keep refining, and keep building tools that genuinely make collaboration easier. Kestrel is more than a product launch—it's a step toward a future where great work isn't slowed down by the tools meant to support it.

We can't wait to see what teams build with it.
